Before you ask

The four questions we get most

Do I need experience?

No. Every trip is graded 1 to 5, and grades 1 and 2 assume you have never done the activity before. Your guide teaches the skills on the day, and nobody gets left behind on a ridge.

What gear is included?

All technical and safety kit: wetsuits, boards, ropes, harnesses, helmets, kayaks, buoyancy aids and group camping equipment. You bring clothes, boots and a sleeping bag — the pack list above covers it.

Can I book on my own?

Most people do. Around seven in ten of our bookings are solo, and trips cap at twelve so the group actually gets to know each other rather than splitting into cliques.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel up to 14 days before departure and you get a full refund, no questions asked. Inside 14 days you can move your place to another date or pass it to a friend.
